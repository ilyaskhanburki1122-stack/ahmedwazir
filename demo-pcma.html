ABOUT PCMA

A Strong Network Of
Chemical Manufacturers

The Pakistan Chemical Manufacturers Association (PCMA) was established to serve as the unified, authoritative voice of the chemical manufacturing sector in Pakistan. Representing basic chemicals, specialty formulations, polymers, fertilizers, industrial gases, and coatings, PCMA advocates for policy reforms, tariff rationalization, and energy security.

By collaborating with government ministries, international chemical councils, and research universities, PCMA drives technological modernization, import substitution, and environmental stewardship across Pakistan.

Policy & Tariff Advocacy

Direct representation before FBR, NTC & Ministry of Commerce.

Global B2B Linkages

Affiliation with ICCA, Gulf Petrochemicals & Asian Chemical Councils.

SPECIALIZED DOMAINS

Committee Area

Specialized technical committees addressing key industrial challenges, legislative reforms and innovation standards.

Tariff, Taxation & Budget

Formulates budget proposals for the Federal Board of Revenue, customs duties adjustments, and sales tax refunds.

Energy & Utilities Affairs

Advocates for uninterrupted gas supply, competitive power tariffs, and captive power policy reforms for chemical plants.

Environment, Health & Safety

Champions Responsible Care® code, OSHA industrial standards, hazard prevention, and effluent discharge compliance.

R&D, Innovation & Academia

Bridges chemical manufacturers with leading universities (NUST, UET, LUMS) for commercialization of local patents.

International Trade & WTO

Monitors anti-dumping duties, FTAs with regional countries, and overseas chemical export facilitation missions.

Quality Standards & PSQCA

Works with Pakistan Standards & Quality Control Authority to harmonize local chemical grades with REACH & GHS.
