About Servis Group

Founded in 1970, Servis Industries Limited is the largest manufacturer and exporter of motorcycle, bicycle, agricultural, and light truck tyres in Pakistan. Operating state-of-the-art automated manufacturing plants in Gujrat and Muridke, we produce millions of tyres annually meeting stringent European (E4) and American (DOT) quality standards.
