FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers to help you get started smoothly.

How long does it take to build a complete website?
Standard landing pages and basic websites are delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Complex e-commerce stores, LMS platforms, or custom web portals usually take between 7 to 14 days depending on feature requirements.
Will my website be mobile friendly and fast loading?
Yes, absolutely! Every website we build is 100% responsive, optimized for smartphones, tablets, and large screens, and passes high Google PageSpeed performance scores.
Do you provide custom design or use ready templates?
We craft custom designs tailored to your brand identity, colors, and industry needs. We do not copy generic templates, ensuring your site stands out from competitors.
What technologies do you use for web development?
We specialize in modern frontend stacks including HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript ES6+, React, Next.js, Node.js Express, as well as WordPress and WooCommerce when requested.
Do you offer website maintenance after launch?
Yes! We provide post-launch support, security audits, performance updates, and minor content revisions to keep your website running smoothly 24/7.
